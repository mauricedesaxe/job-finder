Title: Senior Applied AI Product Engineer

URL Source: https://example.com/jobs/fine-tuning-product-engineer

Markdown Content:
Remote in Europe

Salary: $150,000-$180,000 USD

We build a customer-facing support product that helps operations teams resolve complex cases. You will own AI product features from customer discovery through production and measure their effect on resolution quality.

Your primary modeling work will develop and optimize supervised fine-tuning methods for existing open-source language models. You will curate domain examples, compare parameter-efficient tuning techniques, evaluate resulting behavior, and ship the best existing-model adaptation behind our product APIs.

This role does not train new base or foundation models, invent new model architectures, distill models, or quantize models. You will also build retrieval, tool-use, and evaluation features that customers use directly.

You will work hands-on with Python and TypeScript, partner with product designers and customer teams, and own deployment and monitoring for the features you ship.
